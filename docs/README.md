# AI Teaching Canvas

> AI-native teaching canvas for Indonesian teachers.

## Product idea

A teacher-first teaching platform that allows teachers to prepare and run lessons using:

- voice commands;
- AI-generated structured teaching scenes;
- curriculum-aware lesson generation;
- validated mathematics content;
- digital ink using mouse, touch, or stylus;
- a smartphone as the teacher controller;
- laptop, monitor, TV, projector, or interactive smart board as the classroom display.

The product is **software-first and hardware-agnostic**. A smart board is not required.

## Core product principle

> **Bicara. Tulis. Mengajar. AI mengikuti.**

The teacher remains in control. AI acts as a teaching copilot, not an autonomous teacher.

## Current MVP direction

Initial market scope:

- Indonesia
- Teacher-first
- Mathematics
- Grades 4 SD through 9 SMP

Initial intelligence prototype:

- Grade 5: Fractions
- Grade 7: Basic Algebra / Linear Equations

Minimum classroom setup:

- Laptop or PC
- Monitor / TV / projector
- Teacher smartphone
- Internet connection

Smart board support is an enhancement, not a requirement.
