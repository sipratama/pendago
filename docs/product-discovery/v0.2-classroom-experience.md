# Product Discovery V0.2 — Classroom Experience

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Locked:** 3 September 2026

## 1. Core journey

```text
PREPARE
   ↓
REVIEW / EDIT
   ↓
START CLASSROOM
   ↓
PAIR TEACHER PHONE
   ↓
TEACH
   ↓
ADAPT WITH AI
   ↓
WRITE / ANNOTATE
   ↓
SAVE SESSION
```

## 2. Product surfaces

The experience is organized around three primary surfaces:

1. **Teacher Preparation**
2. **Teacher Smartphone Controller**
3. **Classroom Canvas**

## 3. Teacher Preparation

Preparation supports both:

- AI-generated complete lesson draft;
- teacher modification and refinement.

Locked behavior:

> AI generates a complete lesson draft, while the teacher remains free to change it.

Long-term:

- upload PDF / PPT and convert it into an interactive lesson.

This import feature is **post-MVP**.

## 4. Teacher smartphone as primary teacher device

The teacher identity is centered on the smartphone.

The intended flow:

```text
Teacher logs in on phone
        ↓
Select class
        ↓
Select / prepare lesson
        ↓
Start teaching
        ↓
Pair classroom display
```

The classroom computer does not need to permanently store the teacher account.

This design also enables future teacher-specific:

- teaching history;
- class history;
- attendance workflow;
- preferences.

Attendance is **not part of the initial MVP**.

## 5. Pairing model

Phone and classroom display connect to the same cloud teaching session.

They do **not** use screen mirroring.

They do **not** need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

Example:

```text
Teacher phone ───────┐
                     ▼
                Cloud Session
                     ▲
Classroom laptop ────┘
```

The laptop may use:

- school Wi-Fi;
- teacher hotspot;
- another internet connection.

The teacher phone may independently use mobile data.

## 6. Classroom display

The classroom screen is clean and student-facing.

It must not look like ChatGPT.

No conversation transcript is shown.

AI changes the teaching canvas itself.

## 7. Canvas evolution

Product evolution:

```text
A. AI Presentation
        ↓
C. Hybrid AI Canvas / Whiteboard
```

MVP includes the foundation of the hybrid direction.

## 8. Digital ink

Digital ink is part of MVP.

Users can:

- write;
- highlight;
- erase;
- undo;
- redo;
- clear annotations.

Mouse, touch, and stylus should share one interaction model.

## 9. AI handwriting interpretation

Handwriting understanding is also included in MVP in an explicit/request-driven form.

Example:

1. Student writes a solution on the board.
2. Teacher says: “Periksa jawaban ini.”
3. AI interprets relevant ink.
4. Mathematics validation checks the solution.
5. Result is shown privately to the teacher first.

The system does **not** continuously analyze every stroke.

## 10. Voice behavior

Primary interaction:

- Push-to-talk.

AI automatically distinguishes:

- command;
- dictation;
- question;
- generation request.

AI voice output exists but is **OFF by default**.

AI only speaks when explicitly requested, such as:

- “Bacakan soal.”
- “Jelaskan dengan suara.”

## 11. Private teacher UI

AI progress and suggestions appear privately on the teacher device.

Examples:

- generating content;
- low speech confidence;
- approval preview;
- answer validation;
- curriculum suggestion;
- teaching suggestion.

Student-facing display remains calm and stable.

## 12. AI execution modes

Teacher may choose:

### Auto Mode

High-confidence safe actions execute automatically.

### Approval Mode

Generative results are previewed before they are shown publicly.

Destructive actions still require confirmation.

## 13. Live improvisation

**Live AI improvisation is a killer feature.**

The teacher must be able to adapt in the middle of class:

> “Buat contoh yang lebih sederhana.”

> “Visualisasikan.”

> “Buat soal yang mirip tapi lebih sulit.”

Preparation is important, but live adaptation is the main differentiator from presentation software.

## 14. Student devices

Student smartphones are **not required** for the core product.

The classroom must work in schools where phones are prohibited.

Future optional mode may support:

- QR participation;
- live quiz;
- student devices;
- response analytics.

## 15. Initial visual MVP

Prioritized:

1. Algebra / equations
2. Fractions
3. Basic geometry

Graphing and 3D are post-MVP.

## 16. Target education range

Product scope:

- Grade 4 SD through Grade 9 SMP.

Initial pilot/intelligence validation:

- Grade 5
- Grade 7

## 17. Privacy

Teacher push-to-talk audio is **not stored by default**.

Persist:

- recognized command;
- structured intent;
- relevant action/result.

Do not persist raw audio unless explicitly opted in in a future capability.

## 18. Go-to-market direction

Initial approach:

> **Teacher-first**

The product should be testable by individual teachers before requiring school procurement or institutional rollout.
