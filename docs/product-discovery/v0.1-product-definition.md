# Product Discovery V0.1 — Product Definition

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Locked:** September 2026

## 1. Product definition

The product is an **AI-native teaching canvas / teaching copilot for Indonesian teachers**.

It is not a smart-board hardware product.

It is software that can run on:

- laptop / PC;
- monitor;
- TV;
- projector;
- interactive display;
- smart board.

The smart board is only one possible output/input device.

## 2. Initial target

Primary user:

- Indonesian teachers.

Initial education scope:

- SD;
- SMP;
- SMA as long-term market direction.

Initial subject:

- **Mathematics**.

Initial practical product scope later narrowed to:

- Grade 4 SD through Grade 9 SMP.

## 3. Core interaction

Primary AI interaction:

- **push-to-talk voice input**.

Teacher can also interact traditionally using:

- mouse;
- touch;
- stylus;
- digital ink.

AI should generate teaching content that is:

- correct;
- clear;
- visually attractive;
- appropriate for the selected grade and curriculum context.

## 4. Product experience

The intended experience is not a chatbot.

The teacher should be able to say:

> “Buat tiga soal pecahan untuk kelas lima.”

Then:

> “Yang kedua terlalu mudah.”

Then:

> “Tampilkan pembahasannya langkah demi langkah.”

The classroom display should update directly.

The teacher should feel that they are **teaching**, not operating an AI interface.

## 5. Teacher control

AI does not replace the teacher.

The teacher remains responsible for:

- what is taught;
- whether AI content is shown;
- when explanations are revealed;
- whether AI suggestions are accepted;
- classroom pacing.

## 6. Hardware principle

Minimum viable physical setup:

```text
Teacher Smartphone
        +
Laptop / PC
        +
Monitor / TV / Projector
        +
Internet
```

A smart board is not required.

## 7. Long-term product direction

The long-term product may include:

- hybrid AI whiteboard;
- interactive mathematics visualizations;
- 2D and 3D teaching models;
- teacher history;
- classroom history;
- optional student interaction;
- assessments;
- attendance;
- school-level capabilities.

These are not all part of the initial MVP.

## 8. Core positioning hypothesis

Working product idea:

> **Bicara. Tulis. Mengajar. AI mengikuti.**

English direction:

> **Prepare with AI. Teach naturally. Adapt in real time.**
